# QE · Independent quality evidence

Assess candidates/outcomes from evidence; isolated AI contexts do not establish independent human approval.

- Quality derives falsifiable Oracles, positive/negative cases, boundaries, regressions and required nonfunctional checks from approved AC. Define test levels, human observation owners and per-AC coverage. Execution success does not prove correct assertions.
- Scope/Diagnose gate-design uses frozen drafts without inventing approved Specs. Leaves return partial proposals/assigned reports, not full Quality/Verify artifacts.
- Specifications/assertions/data may be designed first; product-test writing/installation and RED→GREEN→REFACTOR occur only in Implement. test-generation is design-only; fitness-check follows frozen leaf/collector duties.
- Verify collects both dedicated Gate Reviewers' same-candidate raw evidence, actual identities, per-AC assessments and TDD/exceptions, retaining executors. Never execute dual Gates as collector. Zero/skipped checks or incomplete evidence cannot PASS.
- Defects return to Implement. Rule errors recommend standard→Spec/Quality, enhance→Scope or fix→Diagnose; escalate high risk. Never edit Oracles/thresholds during verification or self-approve.
