# Shared Gate invariants

Architecture and Quality require two distinct actual Reviewers, then a fresh collector. All three Agents/runs must differ from each other and every Implement contributor; DEV self-tests cannot substitute. Resource limits serialize, never combine roles.

Verify approved baseline/entry digests/authority and the same complete frozen candidate; enumerate/hash before/after under worker. Missing design/code returns blocked to producers. No product/test/Oracle/rule edits, threshold lowering or self-approval.

Each Gate needs ≥1 relevant nonempty blocking check and all mandatory approved rules passing. Zero/all-skipped/empty/irrelevant assertions, tool failures/timeouts, missing/truncated/unknown raw evidence or candidate drift block. Full reports stay in files; summaries/normalized PASS prove no success. Project tools/permissions enforce CI.

Read only assigned frozen review_cards and source_refs in instruction_refs, once per run. Cards cannot remove approved checks or authorize repair. Findings retain rule/location/candidate/evidence and confirmed/disproved/inconclusive status; uncertainty on a required rule cannot PASS. Selection and structural validation establish neither truth nor Gate success.
