# Compare design options

Follow `core:prompts/common.md`. Use only the approved brief bound as `analysis` and the project baseline. Compare at least two viable options and the current-state option against the brief's criteria: functional fit, maintainability, integration, operability and migration implications. Cite inspectable evidence; label unmeasured capacity, cost and performance estimates as assumptions.

This is independent of the security stage. Do not depend on its unfinished artifact or change the product to test an option. Write only `options-study.md` under the `analysis` contract. Document tradeoffs, disqualifying constraints, evidence gaps and a conditional recommendation. A recommendation does not authorize implementation, procurement or migration.
